Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Explicit policy governing mentorship seats, diagnostic products, and advisory services.

1. Strict Non-Refundable Policy

Due to the proprietary nature of intellectual frameworks, digital tools, time-intensive personal strategy consultations, and strictly limited cohort sizes (maximum 12 seats per intake), all fees paid for the Mastermind, 1-on-1 consultations, and digital frameworks are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable.

2. High-Commitment Standard

Our advisory services are specifically engineered for serious, action-oriented leaders. We do not work with passive spectators. When a seat is confirmed in our cohort, significant executive resources and customized strategy preparation are committed immediately. Therefore, we do not offer trial periods or change-of-mind refunds.

3. Exceptions: Duplicate Charges

Refunds are granted solely in cases of verified technical payment errors, such as an accidental duplicate charge for the exact same transaction. To request a correction, notify our desk within 48 hours of transaction at hello@jagatturkiya.com with transaction receipts.

4. Questions Prior to Enrolling

If you have questions regarding whether the Mastermind or advisory programs are appropriate for your specific financial position, please complete the strategy application and discuss your objectives with our advisory team prior to enrollment.
