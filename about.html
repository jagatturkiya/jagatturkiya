Biography & Vision

The Journey of Jagat Turkiya

From walking on foot for 12 continuous months to save ₹30, to addressing thousands on international platforms and being recognized on the Times Square billboard in New York City.

Humble Beginnings, Relentless Grit

Before stepping onto global seminar stages or writing bestselling literature, Jagat’s life was shaped in the crucible of scarcity. Early in his journey, he walked miles on foot every day for an entire year simply to save ₹30 in daily transit costs. That period forged an unshakeable understanding of human discipline, the value of small compound habits, and the psychological traps of self-pity.

Rather than allowing hardship to become an excuse, Jagat channeled that intensity into studying the mechanics of human behavior, high-performance coaching, and leadership psychology.

"True leadership is not about title or prestige; it is about rising above ego and serving people with absolute clarity."

— Jagat Turkiya

The Core Philosophy

"Think Different, Make Difference"

Transformation is never an accidental event. It is a systematic process governed by four essential disciplines.

01

Radical Clarity

Most individuals do not suffer from lack of effort — they suffer from ambiguity. Clarity means brutally identifying where you truly stand and what is holding you back.

02

Undivided Focus

In a world optimized for continuous distraction, focus is a superpower. Eliminating the non-essential to channel all available energy into key milestones.

03

Disciplined Action

Knowledge without execution is merely illusion. Developing personal systems that ensure relentless daily follow-through regardless of mood or convenience.
