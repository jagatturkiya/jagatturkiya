The Written Word

Published Works by Jagat Turkiya

Five authored titles distilling real-life struggles, deep human psychology, and actionable wisdom into books read by thousands across the globe.

Personal Mastery & Execution

Kill That Black Dragon : Procrastination

Procrastination is not laziness — it is an emotional and psychological trap that robs individuals of their highest potential. In this seminal guide, Jagat breaks down the exact mechanisms of delay, chronic hesitation, and comfort-seeking, offering readers a surgical blueprint to take immediate, fearless action.

  • ◆ Recognizing subconscious fear triggers.
  • ◆ Building the 5-minute action loop.
  • ◆ Structuring an environment that makes discipline effortless.

Inspiring Biographies & Real Life

Asli Nayak - Asal Zindagi Se Asli Kahaniyan

True heroes do not wear capes; they walk amongst us, fighting invisible daily battles against poverty, illness, failure, and self-doubt. Asli Nayak captures the raw, unfiltered journeys of real human beings who rose against impossible odds.

  • ◆ Developing resilience in times of extreme adversity.
  • ◆ Lessons from grassroots triumphs across India.
  • ◆ Discovering the hero within your own life circumstances.

Human Connection & Digital Age

Langotiya Yaar: Digital Dhadkan Aur Dost

In an era dominated by superficial virtual connections, the essence of authentic friendship and human bonding is fading. Langotiya Yaar examines how technology impacts our deepest emotions and how we can preserve genuine warmth, trust, and childhood camaraderie.

  • ◆ Reclaiming presence in an attention-economy world.
  • ◆ The psychology of lifelong loyalty and friendship.
  • ◆ Balancing digital communication with soul-level bonding.

Philosophical Reflections

Anubhav Sagar: Exquisite Life Lessons

Experience is the greatest teacher, but only if one is willing to distill its lessons. Anubhav Sagar compiles years of deep observation, personal triumphs, and coaching insights into timeless principles for inner peace, emotional maturity, and lasting fulfillment.

  • ◆ Turning painful experiences into enduring wisdom.
  • ◆ Developing an unshakeable internal moral compass.
  • ◆ Navigating the seasons of youth, responsibility, and legacy.

Family Harmony & Relationships

Nadar Rishte - Khushhaal Jeevan Ki Kunji?

A flourishing life is hollow if the relationships at home are strained. Nadar Rishte unpacks the delicate art of maintaining love, emotional safety, mutual respect, and patience in modern households and marriage.

  • ◆ Resolving ego-clashes and communication gaps.
  • ◆ The sacred value of attentive listening in families.
  • ◆ Creating a generational atmosphere of joy and trust.
